Adobo

The Heart of Filipino Cuisine

Adobo comes from the Spanish word "adobar", which means "to marinate, season, or dress." The adobo I am specifically talking about is Filipino adobo, where meat, seafood, or even vegetables are browned and then simmered in a rich mixture of vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, bay leaves, and peppercorns.

"A complex, savory flavor profile deeply characterized by a bold tangy punch from vinegar balanced with deep, savory saltiness from soy sauce."

It is my favorite because it is delicious, simple, and affordable to make, as well as easy to preserve. It is truly a comforting dish that can help you get by during hard times.
